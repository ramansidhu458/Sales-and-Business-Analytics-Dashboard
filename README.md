# Sales & Business Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, regional performance, product trends, and shipping operations using the Sample Superstore dataset.

The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights to support better business decision-making.

## Dashboard Pages

- **Sales Overview** – Key performance indicators and overall sales performance.
- **Regional & Customer Analysis** – Regional sales and customer segment analysis.
- **Product & Profitability Analysis** – Top-selling products, profit by sub-category, and category-wise sales and profit.
- **Sales Trend & Time Analysis** – Monthly sales trends, yearly sales, order distribution, and shipping mode analysis.
- **Shipping & Order Analysis** – Shipping performance, delivery time, state-wise sales, and customer order distribution.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Sample Superstore Dataset

## Key Features
- Interactive visualizations
- Sales and profit analysis
- Customer segment analysis
- Regional sales comparison
- Product performance analysis
- Shipping and delivery analysis
- Date-based sales trends

## Objective
To analyze business data through interactive dashboards, identify sales and profitability patterns, and gain useful insights into customer behavior, product performance, and regional business performance.
